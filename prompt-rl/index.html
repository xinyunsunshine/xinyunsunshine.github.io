Posterior sampling through prompts

Posterior sampling

Sample a world model and follow its optimal policy.

Mk∼P(M∣Hk) πk∈argmaxπJMk(π)

PDE

Sample a prompt and follow the VLA policy it induces.

pk∼ρVLM(·∣g,Hk) πk(·∣o)=πθ(·∣o,pk)

Prompt search objective

p⋆∈argmaxp 𝔼[R(τ,g)∣p,πθ]

Both commit to one policy for the episode and use feedback to guide the next sample.
